01

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file that is stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. It allows the site to remember information about your visit — such as your preferred language, the pages you have viewed, or the duration of your session — in order to improve your experience and analyze audience behavior.

Cookies do not contain viruses and cannot execute programs. They are harmless to your device.

02

Types of cookies used

Our site uses two categories of cookies :

  • Strictly necessary cookies: essential for the website to function (remembering your language, session management). These cookies cannot be disabled without affecting the proper functioning of the website. They do not collect any data for commercial or statistical purposes.
  • Analytical cookies (audience measurement) which are only stored with your consent. They enable us to count visits, find out where our visitors come from (country, city) and which pages are most frequently visited. This data is anonymous and is used exclusively to improve our website.

We do not use any advertising cookies or behavioural targeting cookies. Our only audience measurement tool is Google Analytics 4, configured to collect only aggregated and anonymised data.

03

Google Analytics 4 Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to analyse traffic to our website. This tool helps us answer specific questions:

  • How many visits does our website receive each week?
  • Where do our visitors come from (Belgium, France, Switzerland, other countries)?
  • Which pages are the most visited?
  • Which devices (mobile, computer) do our visitors use to browse?

Google Analytics 4 automatically anonymises IP addresses. Your full IP address is never recorded or transmitted to Google.

05

Your Consent

On your first visit, a cookie management banner informs you about the cookies used and asks for your consent to the placement of analytics cookies.

  • If you accept : Google Analytics 4 cookies are activated and begin to collect anonymous browsing data.
  • If you reject or ignore the banner : no analytical cookies are set. We do not receive any browsing data via GA4.

You can withdraw your consent at any time using the Google Analytics opt-out module (see the next section).

06

Manage your cookies

You have several options to control or delete cookies

Through the Google module :
Install the Google Analytics opt-out module for your browser. Once installed, no data will be transmitted to GA4 during your visits.

Through your browser's settings :

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Disabling analytics cookies will not affect your browsing experience on our website in any way. Only the cookies that are strictly necessary (language, consent) will remain active.
